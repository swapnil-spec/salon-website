About The Studio

A neighbourhood salon, run like an atelier

Lumière Studio began as a two-chair corner salon with one promise: treat every client like a regular. Years later, that promise still shapes everything — from the products we choose to the way we listen before the first snip. Our stylists train continuously, our tools are sanitised between every guest, and our chairs are yours for as long as you need them.

Whether you're here for a five-minute trim or a full bridal transformation, you'll leave with more than a new look — you'll leave lighter.
